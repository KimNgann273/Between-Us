# Per-spore variation is fixed at world creation

Every spore carries a `variation`: how big its body is, how heavy its hyphae are drawn, and how many hyphae, how far and how curled they grow when it joins the mycelium. These are rolled once, in the `Spore` constructor, rather than at the moment a spore is joined in Giving. Rolling them at join time would mean calling `random()` from inside `update()`, so the numbers would depend on frame timing and the piece would look different on every run; the spore layout is already pinned by `randomSeed(WORLD.seed)` for exactly this reason, and the screenshots and the recording need the same world twice. The values are derived from a small hash of the spore's own position rather than drawn from the seeded stream, so introducing variation did not shift the layout: the spores sit precisely where seed 11 has always put them. The cost is that variation can never depend on anything that happens during the journey — how far a spore actually reached, who joined it, how long it waited — and that every spore carries numbers it may never use, since a spore that is never joined still holds a hypha count it never spends. We accepted both: nothing in the piece wants join-time context, and being able to reproduce the same world twice is worth more.
