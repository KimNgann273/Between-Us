# One continuous world across all phases

The piece runs as one persistent world. The spore watched in Drifting is the one that grows hyphae in Receiving, and those hyphae stay part of the mycelium in Giving. Moving between phases changes the camera and what the viewer can do, never the world itself. We chose this over a separate scene per phase because the concept is accumulation: entanglement cannot be undone, so the structure must never reset. This makes the build harder (every phase has to keep what the earlier ones left behind, and jumping to a later phase needs a believable world to already exist), but the continuity is the main source of the work's meaning and its innovation marks. Going back to an earlier phase moves the camera and caption only; it never undoes growth. The one way to clear the world is returning Home, which starts the whole piece over rather than rewinding a phase.
