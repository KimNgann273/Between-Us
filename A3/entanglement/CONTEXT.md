# Between Us

An interactive p5.js artwork (OART1033 A3.2) that stages *Entanglement* — its
guiding word, not its title — through the life cycle of fungi: a lone spore
gives up its isolation and becomes part of an interdependent mycelial network.

## Organisms

**Spore**:
A single fungal organism, shown as a point of light. Every hypha grows out of exactly one spore.
_Avoid_: orb, particle, node

**First Spore**:
The spore the viewer follows from the start. It is the most saturated organism in the world, and the first to germinate and fuse.
_Avoid_: your spore, our spore, main spore, player, lone spore

**Dormant**:
The state of a spore that has not germinated yet. Dormancy is about growth, not
appearance: most dormant spores are faint and colourless, but the First Spore is
dormant too, and it already has its colour.
_Avoid_: asleep, inactive, dead

**Germination**:
A spore beginning to grow hyphae. A dormant spore also gains its colour at that moment; the First Spore already has its colour.
_Avoid_: waking, activation, spawning

**Hypha**:
A thread-like tendril growing out of one spore. Plural: hyphae.
_Avoid_: root, branch, tendril, line

**Fork**:
A point where one hypha splits into two, within a single spore's growth.
_Avoid_: junction, branch point

**Cord**:
Several hyphae from one spore travelling toward the same spore together, splitting and rejoining along the way. Only Giving grows them; Receiving's rescue crosses on a single hypha.
_Avoid_: bundle, cable, link, connection, strand (a strand is one hypha within a Cord)

**Fusion**:
The event in which hyphae from two _different_ spores meet and join.
_Avoid_: connection, collision, overlap

**Junction**:
The point a fusion leaves behind. Only junctions can be pulled, and a pull always reaches across more than one organism.
_Avoid_: node, dot, fork

**Mycelium**:
Every spore and hypha joined together through junctions, forming one connected system (the "Wood Wide Web").
_Avoid_: network, web (as a noun for the structure), graph

**Pulse**:
A travelling surge of light carried along hyphae and through junctions.
_Avoid_: signal, ripple, wave

**Nutrient**:
A food source in the soil, placed just beyond the First Spore's reach. The First Spore senses it and grows toward it but cannot reach it; another spore's network does, and passes it on through their fusion.
_Avoid_: attractor, target, food, click point

## Journey

**Home**:
The screen the piece opens on, and the one the Home button returns to. It shows
the First Spore alone, held in the Instrument, under the work's title. Leaving it
always starts a fresh world.
_Avoid_: landing page, title screen, menu, start screen

**Instrument**:
The measuring apparatus drawn over Home: a ruled grid, a crosshair on the First
Spore, and telemetry reading the real world behind it. It belongs to Home alone
and is dismissed the moment the viewer begins; it never returns.
_Avoid_: HUD (that is the chrome inside the journey), overlay, frame

**Narrator**:
The unseen, neutral voice that speaks every caption line to the viewer.
_Avoid_: the mycelium speaking, orb, dialogue partner

**Phase**:
One stage of the journey, each with its own interaction. There are three, in order: Drifting, Receiving, Giving.
_Avoid_: level, scene, step

**Drifting**:
The first phase: the spore is alone and unanchored, and the wider field of spores is revealed.
_Avoid_: Drift (the old name for this phase)

**Receiving**:
The second phase: the First Spore grows toward a Nutrient, runs out of reserves just short of it, and is saved when another spore's network, which reached it first, fuses with its hyphae and passes the nutrients across.
_Avoid_: Reach (the old name for this phase), Connect

**Giving**:
The third phase: the revived First Spore reaches out to the dormant spores around it; each one joined grows a little and reaches on for the next, until the Mycelium spans the world. Pulling one spore flexes the whole network, and none can be pulled free.
_Avoid_: Web (the old name for this phase; "web" is also not a noun for the structure — that is the Mycelium)

**Outro**:
The closing beat after Giving: darkness, then the closing lines one at a time — each arriving whole rather than word by word — and the way back Home under the last of them. It is not a phase and has no tab.
_Avoid_: Phase 4, ending phase
